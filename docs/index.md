# Glade

Glade is a decision-centric governance platform for social purpose organisations — charities, co-operatives, CICs, partnerships, and community groups.

Every part of Glade is built around the **decision** as the primary unit of governance memory. Decisions are traceable, reviewable, and connected to living governance documents, so nothing gets lost to old emails, scattered minutes, or departing trustees.

## What you can do with Glade

- **Log decisions** with full context — who was involved, what method was used, what the rationale was, and what actions follow
- **Track actions** that arise from decisions and see at a glance what's overdue
- **Run live meetings** with a structured facilitation view, speaker stack, timer, and built-in decision flows (consent, voting, advice process, delegation)
- **Maintain living documents** — constitutions, policies, terms of reference — with version history and decision trails showing why each section exists
- **Develop proposals** through structured discussion before they reach a formal decision
- **Surface topics** — questions, tensions, and agenda suggestions — as lightweight governance prompts
- **Visualise your governance** on The Glade, an interactive canvas that maps decisions and their connections
- **Share governance publicly** with per-section transparency controls and embeddable widgets
- **Get AI insights** — pattern analysis, review questions, document impact suggestions, governance digests, and new member briefings (Canopy plan)

## Getting started

When you first sign in and create a space, Glade walks you through a short getting-started checklist:

1. Your space is created automatically
2. Invite a team member
3. Log your first decision
4. Record a meeting

From there, explore the sidebar to find decisions, meetings, documents, proposals, topics, and The Glade.
